Chapter 2 · 2024 – 2026

Financially strengthening the UAE's blue-collar workers

Product Manager, C3Pay · Edenred (French fintech, B2B2C) · partner bank RAK Bank · Dubai
2M+users on C3Pay, a payroll card and wallet
75–80%of salary sent home, leaving almost no buffer
3product lines I owned: C3Pay+, Mobile Recharge, Salary Advance

Most users were migrant workers with low, unpredictable budgets and limited digital literacy. Standard playbooks assume people read, trust and self-serve. Mine didn't, so my strategy was always the same: call the users, find the real cause, test it, and build only what fixes it.

At Edenred I learned to build for people who are easy to overlook. Then Abu Dhabi asked me to build for everyone.
